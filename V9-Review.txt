RIPL V9: consistency and AEO review

WHAT CHANGED
A shared responsive typography scale now controls public page headings, section headings, cards, body copy and CTAs. Desktop hero headings top out at 64px; section headings at 44px; body text is 17px. Mobile hero headings use 34–40px, section headings 28–32px and body text 16px. Business-owner mobile headline line breaks retain their approved treatment. Quiz questions and controls keep their compact interaction scale.

Sections use a common 72px desktop / 48px mobile rhythm. Existing brand colors, gold highlights, approved images and quotes are preserved. Assessment photos retain their explicit height limits.

AEO STRUCTURE
Every page has a connected organization, website and webpage schema graph. Educational question pages include Article and BreadcrumbList markup with RIPL Wealth as the author. Team bio pages use ProfilePage and Person markup with the roles already shown on the site. No credentials, review ratings, outcomes or publication dates were invented.

Question pages have visible breadcrumbs. The sitemap includes all four team bios. Social preview metadata includes the approved homepage image, or the person's portrait on their bio page. Repetitive question-page descriptions now summarize the opening answer.

AEO CONTENT
Six Money Questions articles replace generic explanations and hypothetical examples with topic-specific guidance, relevant internal links and IRS or SEC references. Existing opening answers and approved marketing copy remain. Structured note articles add an SEC reference. Minor capitalization errors are corrected.

CHECKS

All pages passed checks for exactly one H1, unique titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, valid JSON-LD, working local links and fragments, existing images and alt attributes. Sitemap: 60 unique valid URLs; the 404 and thank-you pages are excluded. Assessment JavaScript passed Node syntax validation. Shared and assessment CSS passed parsing checks.
