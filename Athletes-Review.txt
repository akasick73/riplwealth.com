CONTENT DIRECTION
Audience: college athletes earning NIL income and professional athletes, with language aimed at people encountering substantial financial responsibilities for the first time.
Approach: emphasize agency, clarity, time and relationships. Avoid shame, financial disaster statistics, stereotypes, forced slang and claims of existing athlete clients or league affiliations. The psychological framing is an editorial recommendation, not a tested conversion claim.

Hero: Your career is taking off. Give your money a game plan.
Key distinction: You can be great at your sport and still be new to this.
Services: spending and cash flow; tax coordination; saving and investing; business and endorsement decisions; career changes; giving and money requests.
Standout section: Let us be the “bad guy.” An optional, permission-based way to communicate the financial boundaries the athlete chooses, without disclosing their balances.
Closing: Bring your questions. We’ll start there.
